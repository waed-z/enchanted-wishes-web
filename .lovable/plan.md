# Birthday Surprise Experience

## Goal
Create a polished, immersive one-page birthday surprise that unfolds through cinematic interactions and remains easy to personalize.

## Experience
- Build an animated opening reveal, birthday centerpiece, scrapbook memories, interactive envelope letter, wish-making cake, surprise gift, admiration cards, Polaroid wall, and emotional finale.
- Connect sections with restrained scroll reveals, floating hearts and sparkles, gentle depth, and a replay flow.
- Add optional music controls with a replaceable audio source and a dreamy pink / midnight pink theme switch.

## Personalization
- Keep the birthday name, messages, captions, admiration notes, photo slots, and music source together in one clearly labeled configuration object.
- Use elegant photo placeholders until real images are supplied.

## Quality
- Use a cohesive blush, rose, cream, burgundy, and lavender design system with script and modern typography.
- Support keyboard and touch interaction, reduced-motion preferences, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow.
- Verify the full journey on desktop and mobile, including opening, envelope, cake, gift, gallery, theme, music, and replay.

## Technical details
- Implement with React, Tailwind CSS, CSS keyframes, Intersection Observer, and small canvas/DOM particle bursts where appropriate.
- Keep animation layers lightweight and disable or simplify them for reduced motion.
